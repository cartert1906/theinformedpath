Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The Informed Path ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit theinformedpath.com or purchase a course, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect very little information directly. When you purchase a course, the following happens:

  • Your email address and payment information are collected and processed by our checkout provider, Payhip, and our payment processor, Stripe — not stored on our own servers.
  • If you email us (support@theinformedpath.com), we receive whatever information you choose to include in that email, handled through our email provider, Zoho Mail.
  • We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels on theinformedpath.com itself. Our checkout page (hosted by Payhip) may use its own cookies for cart and payment functionality — see Payhip's privacy policy for details on that.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver the course files you purchased
  • To respond to support requests or refund requests
  • To send you purchase confirmations and, if applicable, updates about the course you bought

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Payhip — handles checkout, payment collection, and file delivery
  • Stripe — processes payments; we never see or store your full card details
  • Zoho Mail — hosts our support email inbox
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data they process on our behalf.

Data Retention

We retain purchase and support email records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide customer support, handle refund requests, and meet basic recordkeeping obligations. You can request deletion of your information at any time (see "Your Rights" below), though some payment records may be retained by Stripe/Payhip independently for their own legal and accounting requirements.

Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing support@theinformedpath.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law (for example, under the California Consumer Privacy Act or similar state laws) — contact us and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.

Children's Privacy

This course and website are not directed at, and not intended for use by, anyone under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@theinformedpath.com.
