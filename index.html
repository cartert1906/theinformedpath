Why This Exists

Most people have never actually read their credit report.

Your credit report determines whether you get approved for a mortgage, a car loan, or even an apartment — yet most people have never been taught how to read one, what their rights are under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), or how to identify when something on their report doesn't look right.

The Informed Path exists to close that gap. This is not a service that disputes your credit on your behalf — it's a course that teaches you how to do it yourself, step by step, using consumer protections that already belong to you under federal law.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is this credit repair?

No. Credit repair organizations dispute items on your behalf for a fee. The Informed Path teaches you how to understand your credit report and exercise your own dispute rights — you take every action yourself.

Will this improve my credit score?

We can't promise that, and you should be skeptical of anyone who does. This course helps you understand your report accurately and correct genuine errors using the process federal law already provides.

How long does the dispute process take?

Credit bureaus generally have 30 days (sometimes 45) to investigate a dispute under the FCRA. The course covers realistic timelines at each stage.

Do I need any special skills?

No. The course is written in plain language and assumes no prior knowledge of credit reporting or consumer law.
